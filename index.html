SEU TechXplore 2026

The Annual Technology Event of Southeast University — Department of Computer Science and Engineering

SEU TechXplore is a two-day technology festival organized by the Department of Computer Science and Engineering at Southeast University. The event brings together students, faculty members, and industry professionals to celebrate innovation in software, hardware, and emerging technologies. Participants get the chance to attend workshops, compete in contests, and showcase their own projects to a wider audience.

The main goals of the event are to encourage hands-on learning outside the classroom, to strengthen collaboration between students from different departments, and to give young developers early exposure to real industry mentors. Every session, from the beginner-friendly workshops to the advanced hackathon track, is designed with these goals in mind.

Participation is open to all currently enrolled SEU students, and selected sessions are also open to students from other universities. There is no participation fee for students who register using their official SEU student ID. Seats in workshops are limited, so early registration through the form below is strongly recommended.

Tracks and Participation Rules

Available Tracks

Participants may register for one or more of the following tracks:

  • Web & App Development
  • Artificial Intelligence and Machine Learning
  • Competitive Programming
  • Robotics and IoT
  • Cybersecurity Challenge
  • Project Showcase

Registration Steps

Follow these steps in order to complete your registration:

  1. Read the track descriptions and choose your preferred track(s).
  2. Fill in the registration form in the section below with accurate information.
  3. Select your preferred participation date and confirm your team preference.
  4. Submit the form and wait for a confirmation email from the organizing committee.

General Rules (Grouped by Category)

The rules below are grouped into eligibility rules and conduct rules, shown as a nested list:

  • Eligibility Rules
    • Only currently enrolled students may participate in the Hackathon track.
    • Each participant must register with a valid Student ID.
    • Teams may have a maximum of four members.
  • Conduct Rules
    • Plagiarized or pre-built project submissions are not allowed.
    • Participants must remain respectful toward organizers, mentors, and peers.
    • Any form of misconduct may lead to disqualification.
